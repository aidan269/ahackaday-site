## What happened
Attackers redirected SMS delivery in specific carrier pathways and combined it with phishing kits.

## Why this matters beyond one victim
SMS remains a default second factor in many sectors, so telecom-layer abuse has wide downstream effects.

## Technical notes
Passkey and app-based authenticator adoption markedly reduced successful takeovers.
